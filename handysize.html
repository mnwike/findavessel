Handysize: size, capacity and trades

A Handysize is a small geared bulk carrier of roughly 10,000 to 40,000 deadweight tonnes. It reaches ports and berths that larger ships cannot.

Key facts

Handysize
Deadweight10,000 to 40,000 tonnes
Holds and gearFour or five holds, cranes of 25 to 35 tonnes
Typical length and beamAbout 150 to 180 m by 24 to 30 m
Tanker equivalentHandysize tankers, 10,000 to 35,000 tonnes
Next size upHandymax

What Handysizes carry

Minor bulks and parcels that do not fill a larger ship: steel, logs and timber, fertiliser, grain, cement, sugar, scrap and bagged goods. Many have box-shaped holds or log fittings for specific trades.

Where they trade

Everywhere, and especially on regional routes: within the Mediterranean and Black Sea, around South-east Asia, across the Caribbean and along the coasts of Africa and South America. Shallow draught and their own cranes let them use small ports.

Why the class suits first-time owners and charterers

Ships cost less, cargoes are smaller and counterparties are often small and mid-sized companies. That also means more unknown names on both sides, which is where checking the counterparty and securing freight matter most.
