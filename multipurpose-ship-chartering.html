Multipurpose and heavy-lift ship chartering

Multipurpose and heavy-lift ships carry what does not fit a bulk carrier or a container: project cargo, machinery, wind turbine parts, steel, timber and bagged goods.

What a multipurpose ship carries

Project cargo and heavy lifts, steel products and pipes, wind turbine components, timber and logs, bagged cargo, vehicles and small bulk parcels.

Sizes

There are no settled class names for these ships. What matters is deadweight, hold and deck dimensions, and above all the lifting or ramp capacity.

How they are chartered

GENCON for general cargo and HEAVYLIFTVOY for heavy lifts; NYPE or BALTIME for time charters. Freight is usually a lump sum, sometimes per tonne or per cubic metre. For the difference between the two, see voyage and time charters.

What an owner needs from you to quote

  • A packing list: each piece with its weight and dimensions
  • The heaviest and largest single piece
  • Whether cargo may be carried on deck
  • Lifting gear needed, if the ports have none
  • Load and discharge ports
  • Laycan

An enquiry with all of this can be priced without a phone call. One without it usually goes unanswered.
