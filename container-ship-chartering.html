Container ship chartering

Whole container ships are chartered by liner operators and regional carriers, almost always by the day. This is not the same as booking a container on someone else’s service.

What a container ship carries

Containers of every kind, including refrigerated boxes where the ship has reefer plugs. The charterer runs the service and fills the ship.

How they are chartered

NYPE or BOXTIME time charters, for periods from a few months to several years. Hire is quoted in US dollars per day. For the difference between the two, see voyage and time charters.

What an owner needs from you to quote

  • Capacity needed in TEU, and at 14 tonnes per container
  • Number of reefer plugs
  • Whether cranes are needed for ports without gantries
  • Speed and fuel consumption required
  • Delivery and redelivery places and dates
  • The intended service or trading area

An enquiry with all of this can be priced without a phone call. One without it usually goes unanswered.
