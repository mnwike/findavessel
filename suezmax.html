Suezmax: size, capacity and trades

A Suezmax is the largest tanker that can pass through the Suez Canal fully laden: roughly 120,000 to 200,000 deadweight tonnes, carrying about one million barrels.

Key facts

Suezmax
Deadweight120,000 to 200,000 tonnes
Typical cargoAbout 1 million barrels of crude oil
Typical length and beamAbout 275 m by 48 m
What sets the limitSuez Canal draught, about 20.1 m
Next size downAframax
Next size upVLCC

Why the canal defines the class

The Suez Canal Authority limits how deep a ship may sit, and the allowed beam narrows as draught increases. A Suezmax is designed to the largest hull that passes with a full cargo. The limits have been raised as the canal has been deepened, so the class has grown over time.

Where Suezmaxes trade

West Africa to Europe and the Americas, the Black Sea and Mediterranean, and the Middle East to Europe through the canal. A million-barrel parcel is a common size for crude sales, which keeps the class busy.

How they are chartered

Voyage charters priced in Worldscale are the norm, with time charters to oil companies and traders for longer cover. Vetting requirements are close to those for VLCCs.
