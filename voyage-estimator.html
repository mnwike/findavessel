What time charter equivalent means

Time charter equivalent, or TCE, is the daily earning of a voyage after the costs the owner pays on a voyage charter: fuel, port charges, canal tolls and commissions. It lets an owner compare a voyage at USD 26 a tonne with a time charter at USD 14,000 a day, or one cargo with another.

TCE = (freight − commission − fuel − port and other voyage costs) ÷ voyage days

Why charterers should care

If you know roughly what an owner's sum looks like, you know whether your freight idea is realistic. A cargo that works out below the daily rate ships are earning elsewhere will not attract offers.

Limits

You need to supply the distances: this page does not hold a sea distance table. Use a routed distance, not a straight line, and allow for weather and waiting. Daily running costs such as crew and insurance are not deducted, because TCE is measured before them.
