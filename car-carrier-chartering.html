Vehicle carrier and ro-ro chartering

Vehicle carriers and ro-ro ships load cargo on wheels over a ramp: cars, trucks, trailers and heavy machinery. Whole ships are mostly hired on time charter.

What a vehicle carrier carries

New and used cars, trucks, buses, trailers, construction and farm machinery, and other rolling or towable cargo.

Sizes

There are no settled class names for these ships. What matters is deadweight, hold and deck dimensions, and above all the lifting or ramp capacity.

How they are chartered

NYPE or BALTIME time charters, with lump-sum voyage charters for single shipments. Capacity is measured in car equivalent units (CEU) and lane metres. For the difference between the two, see voyage and time charters.

What an owner needs from you to quote

  • The mix of units: how many cars, how many high and heavy
  • The heaviest and tallest unit, for ramp and deck limits
  • Load and discharge ports
  • Dates or period
  • Whether units are new or used, which affects handling and insurance

An enquiry with all of this can be priced without a phone call. One without it usually goes unanswered.
