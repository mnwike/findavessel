Chartering a ship to or from Syria

A whole-ship charter needs the cargo, the quantity, both ports and a window of loading dates. With those an owner can price the voyage. If this is your first, read how to charter a ship, check which size of ship the cargo needs, and look up the terms you will meet: laycan, laytime and demurrage.
