LR2: size, capacity and trades

An LR2, or Long Range 2, is a product tanker of roughly 80,000 to 120,000 deadweight tonnes. It is an Aframax-sized tanker with coated tanks for refined products.

Key facts

LR2
Deadweight80,000 to 120,000 tonnes
Typical cargo600,000 to 750,000 barrels of refined products
Typical length and beamAbout 245 m by 44 m
Crude-trading nameAframax
Next size downLR1
Next size upSuezmax

Where LR2s trade

The longest product routes: naphtha from the Middle East to Japan and Korea, and diesel and jet fuel from the Middle East and India to Europe. The economics are the same as for crude: the longer the voyage, the more a large ship saves per barrel.

LR2 or Aframax

Same hull. Owners switch between clean and dirty trading depending on which market pays more, but switching back to clean is slow and costly. See the Aframax page for the crude side.
