Gas carrier chartering

Gas carriers move liquefied gases under pressure, refrigeration or both: LPG, ammonia, petrochemical gases and LNG. Here is what they carry, the sizes available and what an owner needs to quote.

What a gas carrier carries

Propane and butane (LPG), ammonia, ethylene, ethane, propylene and other petrochemical gases, and LNG on dedicated carriers.

How they are chartered

ASBAGASVOY or GASVOY for voyages; SHELLTIME and SHELLLNGTIME for time charters. LPG freight is quoted in dollars per tonne; LNG carriers are mostly hired by the day. For the difference between the two, see voyage and time charters.

What an owner needs from you to quote

  • Cargo grade and specification
  • Quantity in tonnes
  • Load and discharge ports
  • Laycan
  • Required condition of the tanks on arrival, for example under vapours of the same cargo
  • Vetting requirements

An enquiry with all of this can be priced without a phone call. One without it usually goes unanswered.
