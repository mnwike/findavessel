Laytime and demurrage calculator

Enter the cargo, the rate and the times. The calculator shows the laytime allowed, the time used and the demurrage or despatch that follows.

Laytime allowed is quantity divided by rate. Or enter days directly below.

Leave empty to use quantity and rate.

Weather, holidays or other periods your charter party does not count.

How the sum is done

  1. Laytime allowed is the quantity divided by the daily rate, unless a fixed number of days is agreed.
  2. Time used runs from the start of laytime to completion of cargo work, less any excluded time.
  3. If time used is greater, the difference multiplied by the demurrage rate is owed to the owner.
  4. If it is less, despatch may be owed to the charterer, usually at half the demurrage rate.

What this calculator does not do

It treats one port at a time and takes your word for the start time and the excluded hours. Real laytime statements turn on the charter party wording: when notice of readiness was valid, turn time, whether Sundays and holidays count, and whether time is reversible between ports. Read laytime explained for those terms, and use the statement of facts as your source.
