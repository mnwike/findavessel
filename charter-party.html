What is a charter party?

A charter party is the contract between a shipowner and a charterer for the use of a ship. Nearly all are built on a standard printed form with agreed changes.

The standard forms

FormUsed forNotes
GENCONDry cargo voyage chartersPublished by BIMCO. The 1994 and 2022 editions are both in use.
NYPEDry cargo time chartersNew York Produce Exchange form. The 1946, 1993 and 2015 editions all appear.
ASBATANKVOYTanker voyage chartersThe most widely used tanker voyage form.
SHELLVOY, BPVOYTanker voyage chartersOil company forms, used when that company charters.
SHELLTIMETanker and gas time chartersSets detailed speed and consumption warranties.
NORGRAIN, SYNACOMEXGrain voyage chartersTrade-specific forms.

Recap first, charter party after

Deals are agreed in a negotiation, not by signing a document. When the last term is agreed and the subjects are lifted, the broker issues a recap: a message setting out every agreed term. The contract exists from that moment. The full charter party is drawn up afterwards and sometimes never signed at all, which is why an accurate recap matters so much.

What the main clauses cover

  • The ship: name, size, flag, class, and for time charters its speed and fuel consumption.
  • The cargo and ports: what, how much, where, and that berths must be safe.
  • Laycan: the arrival window and the right to cancel.
  • Freight or hire: the rate, when it is earned and when it is paid.
  • Laytime and demurrage: voyage charters only.
  • Commission: who is paid what, and by whom.
  • Law and arbitration: most often English law with arbitration in London, or US law in New York.
  • Sanctions, war risks and piracy: who bears those risks.

"Subject details"

Main terms are often agreed "subject to details", meaning the remaining charter party wording still has to be settled. Under English law there is usually no binding contract until that subject is lifted. US practice can treat main terms as binding earlier. Know which law your charter is under.
